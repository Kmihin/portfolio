# Portfolio site, Kristjan Mihin

Personal portfolio and freelance site. Owner: Kristjan Mihin, full-stack developer (.NET / Azure / React), Slovenia.

## Stack
- Astro (static output), Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.
- Client JS: three, gsap (ScrollTrigger), lenis, loaded via <script> in Astro components. Keep scripts small and commented. No UI framework on the client.
- Design (v9, "rooms", from my v4 design: ivory paper, navy, brass; Bodoni Moda display + Geist body, self-hosted): the home page is a sequence of full-screen rooms, each with a marble and brass sculpture (blue marble, a deep blue (#172a40) broken into facets and crossed by gold seams of uneven width and hairline cracks, like the background pictures; white marble with faint grey veins; light brass) in an empty slot of its layout (`[data-sculpture]`), drawn by one fixed WebGL canvas behind the page (`src/scripts/sculptures.ts`, three.js; the canvas is layer -1, the rooms' pictures -2, text on top). The sculptures are modelled in Blender (`tools/blender/sculptures.py`, `build.py export` -> `public/models/sculptures.glb`); the site draws the marble with its own shader by material name ("Navy marble", "White marble", "Brass", "Polished brass"). Rooms: hero (the name large on the left, an armillary sphere on the right: a floating globe (no pin) with blue marble seas and brass continents, from `tools/blender/globe-mask.png` (Natural Earth land, public domain), turning on a tilted axis and rising and sinking a little inside brass rings that turn into place on load and then keep turning slowly round it), 01 About (my cut-out portrait in a brass circle in the middle, text left, values right, the figures in a row below counting up, then my path of education and work from `path` in `content/profile.md`), 02 Selected Work (index left, the picked case study's sculpture in the middle, details right: a marble scroll stood up towards the viewer, a floating marble island under a brass cloud, an ornate brass key that turns a quarter, a dumbbell with marble heads and a marble apple, a marble beach court with a brass net, a hollow marble chest whose lid opens on two brass hinges, a modern villa with a front door, a white marble lighthouse for Project Hub whose brass lamp turns slowly and whose door opens), 03 Tech Expertise (a seven-sided navy marble prism with one skill engraved in brass per face; it turns to the picked skill), 04 What I Offer (a brass balance whose beam settles level as the room arrives; its pans hold a pair of objects for the picked service, `PAN_LOADS` in sculptures.py: browser window and gear, browser window and fountain pen, cracked old tablet and new tablet, plug and socket, server rack and cloud, old clock and chip, phone and bell, calendar and key; the pair swaps and the beam sways and settles), the closing statement (a navy marble chess king), Contact with the footer (a white marble envelope whose brass KM seal is pressed onto the flap as the room arrives). Lists move on to the next item every 15 seconds while their room is on screen (a brass line under the picked entry fills up until then; `interval` on Showcase), or when an entry or arrow is clicked (`showcase.ts`, `data-showcase` names the room's sculpture). In the work room the last object sinks away before the next one rises (never two at once). Nothing turns just to turn; every sculpture leans a little towards the pointer. "Full case study" (every study has one; a live site or the source sits beside it) enters the object (`entrance.ts` + `enterWork` in sculptures.ts): the object plays its own move (the chest opens wide, the villa's door opens, the key sinks into the keyhole and turns and a door in its pedestal opens, the ball flies over the net, the dumbbell lifts, the cloud lifts off the servers, the lighthouse's door opens), the view zooms into its `Entry` empty (from the Blender model), a veil in the colour of the object's inside (`#veil`, blue or ivory, `TONES` in entrance.ts) covers the page and the case page opens out of it. The canvas and the veil are kept across client navigation (`transition:persist`). Case study pages show the study's sculpture beside their header. One continuous marble background: my pictures in `public/backgrounds/`, one per room (`.bg-hero`, `.bg-about`, `.bg-work`, `.bg-skills`, `.bg-offer`, `.bg-closing`, `.bg-contact` in global.css), at 40% opacity, each reaching into its neighbours and fading out at top and bottom; rooms must not create a stacking context. Header: KM monogram, the room links, "Let's talk"; transparent at the top, glass after, hides on scroll down. Motion (`motion.ts`): Lenis, `data-split`, `data-rule`, `.reveal` / `.reveal-item`, `data-enter`. Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content lives in `content/`; never hardcode case-study text in components.
- Copy rules: no em/en dashes in visible text (use commas, colons, periods, or a plain hyphen for ranges); the hero eyebrow, chapter numbers and the scroll mark from my mockup are intended.
- Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages (static). Keep the build host-agnostic.

## Code conventions
- Simple, readable code. No clever abstractions.
- No inline styles. Tailwind utility classes or scoped component CSS.
- Small components, one responsibility each.
- Type all frontmatter with Astro content collections (`src/content.config.ts`).
- Two languages: English at `/`, Slovenian under `/sl/` (`src/lib/i18n.ts`: `langOf`, `homePath`, `otherPath`, `copy`). The home page's copy and every UI label are in `content/home/en.json` and `content/home/sl.json`; case studies, skills and services in `content/` (English) and `content/sl/` (Slovenian, the same files translated, collections `projectsSl`, `professionalSl`, `skillsSl`, `servicesSl`; `src/lib/content.ts` picks them by language). Keep strings in content, not in markup; a change to the text is made in both languages. The header has a language switch to the same page in the other language.

## Content rules
- The transport / logistics field I work in now is never named (I cannot take projects in that field): the e-CMR work is shown in general terms as `document-platform.md` ("Digital document exchange platform"), the ReBAC work as "Security, roles and rights for a B2B platform"; the carrier import stays hidden (`_carrier-data-import.md`, skipped by the collections). The apartment site is not named ("Holiday apartment website").
- Professional work (Trinet) is described anonymously: no client names, no screenshots with real data, no internal system names beyond what is in `content/professional/`.
- Side projects may link to GitHub repos.
- AI-assisted scaffolding (Lovable) is disclosed where used.

## Pages
- `/` is the whole site, one scroll of rooms: `#home` hero -> `#about` 01 About me (with the figures) -> `#work` 02 Selected Work -> seam -> `#skills` 03 Tech Expertise -> seam -> `#offer` 04 What I Offer -> `#closing` statement -> `#contact` 05 contact and footer. Skills come from `content/skills.json` (the prism's engraved labels follow its order, short names in `SKILL_LABELS` in sculptures.py), services from `content/services.json`, work from the case-study collections (`label` is the short index name; each study's sculpture is named by its slug).
- `/work/[slug]` and `/sl/work/[slug]`: the case study pages (`CaseStudy.astro`), linked from every study as "Full case study"; the home pages are `Home.astro` for each language.
- The CV (`public/cv/kristjan-mihin-cv-en.pdf`, `-sl.pdf`, linked in Contact) and the share image (`public/og.png`, 1200 x 630) are made from the content by `tools/print/build.py` (`python3 build.py cv`, `python3 build.py share` with the dev server running); rebuild them after changing the about text, the path, the case studies or the skills.
