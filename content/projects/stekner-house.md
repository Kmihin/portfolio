## Problem
The owner of a holiday apartment needed a simple website of their own: rooms, location and a way for guests to get in touch, without paying for a website builder every month.

## What I did
- Built the site in React on a responsive Bootstrap layout: home, rooms, location and contact.
- A contact form that sends straight to the owner's email (EmailJS), with no server to run.
- An embedded map with the apartment and the surroundings.
- Deployment on Firebase Hosting; content updates and maintenance since 2023.

## Result
Live and in use by the owner since 2023, at no hosting cost.
