# Portfolio site, Kristjan Mihin

Personal portfolio and freelance site. Owner: Kristjan Mihin, full-stack developer (.NET / Azure / React), Slovenia.

## Stack
- Astro (static output), Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.
- Client JS: three, gsap (ScrollTrigger), lenis, loaded via <script> in Astro components. Keep scripts small and commented. No UI framework on the client.
- Design (v13, "journey": a light, high-tech site where scrolling travels through a 3D space, after the camera path of noomo's ValenTime): one fixed full-screen Three.js canvas behind every page (`src/scripts/journey.ts`, `#scene` in `Base.astro`). White space (#f5f6f9) with exponential fog, a hemisphere light and a sun, a faint `RoomEnvironment` reflection, flat-shaded white "plaster" geometry with the blue accent as light. It opens on a terminal screen (a canvas texture: whoami, stack, ./enter --scroll) in a white bezel; scrolling shatters it into triangle shards that fly past the camera (all in a vertex shader: `uShatter`, per-shard direction and spin), and the camera flies through and on along one continuous path (`CatmullRomCurve3` for the camera and one for the look point, `centripetal`) past a chain of floating islands, one per chapter: hexagonal slabs with a glowing rim, a rough underside and loose blocks drifting under them, bobbing gently, linked by cables (`TubeGeometry`) with blue and coral pulses. The islands: about (a monolith with my name, a ring of light turning around it), facts (four hexagonal pillars that grow as you arrive, the figures floating above as sprites), work (a screen per case study in two rows, standing up one after another as you arrive, lit faintly), skills (a tower of labelled cubes that opens into a spiral as you arrive), offer (a gyroscope of rings around a blue core with satellites, spinning faster as you arrive), closing (216 cubes that assemble into one block while the section scrolls), contact (an antenna sending out rings of signal). Small square "bits" drift through the air (points). `STOPS` in journey.ts: each island's position, the camera position and the look point relative to it (a look point left of the island puts it on the right of the screen, beside the text; phones look straight at it). Every section carries `data-scene="name"`; `motion.ts` (`followStops`) takes the section whose top has passed mid-screen and the next one, and the camera eases along the path to that point (`presence` per island, 1 at its stop, drives the arrival animations). Labels come from `#scene-data` (JSON on the home page: skills titles, case study labels, the figures); case pages ("detail") look at the work island with plain screens. Pixel ratio capped (2, phones 1.5), nothing drawn while the tab is hidden; reduced motion: the camera holds the first stop. Over the canvas a white haze (`.haze`) on the left and along the top and bottom (all over on phones), so text sits on calm ground; content sits in the left 62% on wide screens. Pinned scenes (`data-pin="200"` pins for 200% of the viewport, scrubbed; only when the section fits the screen): inside, `[data-out]` fades out over the first half and `[data-in]` fades in over the second (the hero: the name gives way to the statement while the screen shatters). Preloader (`#preloader`): a monospace counter 000 to 100 ("Entering the system"; at least 1.4 s and not before the first frame), then the curtain lifts and the hero stages in (`data-enter`). Custom cursor (`#cursor`, fine pointers only, shown from the first move): a blue dot and a trailing ring that grows and turns blue over links (`.is-hot`); the system cursor is hidden while it runs. Palette (global.css): off-white, white surfaces, graphite ink (#0b1220), muted (#5a6577), one electric blue accent (#2f5bff, soft #e6ecff), coral (#ff6a3d) as a signal colour. Fonts: Geist (self-hosted, the only webfont; headings weight 600, tight tracking; `<em>` in a heading is the one word in the accent, upright) and the system monospace stack for labels (`.label`, `.label-mark` adds a small blue square), section numbers, numbers and tags. Surfaces: `.glass` (white translucent panel, square corners, crop marks in two corners), `.shade` (a soft light halo behind free text), `.scene` (a full-screen section that can be pinned). Square buttons (`.btn-primary` graphite, blue with a glow on hover; `.btn-secondary` white outlined). Header (`Header.astro`): the name, the chapter counter in the middle ("02 / 08  Work", from `data-chapter-name`; hidden on phones) and "Let's talk"; hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up; in-page links scroll smoothly (capture-phase click handler). Motion hooks: `data-split` (words rise out of a clipped line; keeps an `<em>` child on its words), `data-rule`, `.reveal` / `.reveal-item`, `data-enter`, `data-count` (numbers count up when they enter). `html` has `overflow-x: clip`. Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content lives in `content/`; never hardcode case-study text in components.
- Copy rules: no em/en dashes in visible text (use commas, colons, periods, or a plain hyphen for ranges); the monospace status lines, chapter numbers and the scroll cue are intended.
- Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages (static). Keep the build host-agnostic.

## Code conventions
- Simple, readable code. No clever abstractions.
- No inline styles. Tailwind utility classes or scoped component CSS.
- Small components, one responsibility each.
- Type all frontmatter with Astro content collections (`src/content.config.ts`).
- English UI. Slovenian translation may come later; keep strings in content, not in markup.

## Content rules
- The transport / logistics field I work in now is never named (I cannot take projects in that field): the e-CMR work is shown in general terms as `document-platform.md` ("Digital document exchange platform"), the ReBAC work as "Security, roles and rights for a B2B platform"; the carrier import stays hidden (`_carrier-data-import.md`, skipped by the collections). The apartment site is not named ("Holiday apartment website").
- Professional work (Trinet) is described anonymously: no client names, no screenshots with real data, no internal system names beyond what is in `content/professional/`.
- Side projects may link to GitHub repos.
- AI-assisted scaffolding (Lovable) is disclosed where used.

## Pages
- `/` is the whole site, one journey: `#home` hero (pinned 200%; the terminal screen on the right shatters while the name gives way to the statement with the buttons; the stack and a scroll cue at the bottom) -> `#about` 01 About me (pinned when it fits; the monolith island: portrait, heading, three paragraphs, four values) -> `#facts` (pinned 120%; the pillar island, the same figures in four panels counting up) -> `#work` 02 Selected work (the screens island: `ChapterHead.astro` + `WorkList.astro`, rows linking to the case study pages) -> `#skills` 03 Tech expertise (the cube tower: `Expertise.astro`, panels from `content/skills.json`) -> `#offer` 04 What I offer (the gyroscope: `Offer.astro`, a numbered list from `content/services.json`) -> `#closing` (pinned 150%; the cubes assemble into one block, the statement on a panel) -> `#contact` 05 Let's talk (the antenna: email button, links, footer line; the home page passes `footer={false}` to Base).
- Work comes from the case-study collections (featured first, then most recent).
- `/work/[slug]` is a page for every case study (stop detail: the work island far behind): label, title, blue tagline and summary, the cover, the feature grid (`CaseFeatures.astro`), a sticky panel with role, stack tags and links beside the markdown body; a "Next" band and `Footer.astro`.
