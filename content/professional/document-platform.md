## Problem
Business documents that pass between several companies were still printed, signed by hand at every step, scanned and re-typed. The goal was one digital document that every party can create, sign and exchange electronically, following an international standard, and reachable from the partners' own systems.

## What I did
- Designed the solution from the standard's documentation and the functional specification, together with the team.
- Data model and backend for the document lifecycle: creation, signing by several parties, status changes, audit trail.
- Multilingual PDF output of the signed document with embedded signatures.
- REST API for partner integrations (document creation, signing flow, roles) and support for partners integrating it.
- Deployment and operations on Azure.

## Result
In production, 10k+ documents a year, used by companies across Europe; also used in cross-company industry pilots.
