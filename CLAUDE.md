# Portfolio site, Kristjan Mihin

Personal portfolio and freelance site. Owner: Kristjan Mihin, full-stack developer (.NET / Azure / React), Slovenia.

## Stack
- Astro (static output), Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.
- Client JS: three, gsap (ScrollTrigger), lenis, loaded via <script> in Astro components. Keep scripts small and commented. No UI framework on the client.
- Design (v7, "ivory paper, navy marble, brass", from my own mockup on top of the v5 structure and motion): the page is warm ivory paper (#f6f0e5 area, shader stone #f9f5ed / #f1e9db with faint gold veins) with deep navy ink (#0d172b) and brass (#a6814e on ivory, #c9a86a on navy) for the surname, italic second lines of headings, chapter numbers on a short brass line, the main button, link underlines and icons. Every section is ivory now; the midnight theme (#121f3a / deep #070c19, ivory text) stays available in the CSS and motion code. Section backgrounds (`public/backgrounds/`, my own images, upscaled 4x with Real-ESRGAN, assigned per section in CSS as a `::before` layer: `.section-bg` plus `.bg-about`, `.bg-projects`, `.bg-tech`, `.bg-services`, `.bg-contact`, and `.footer-bg`; each fades out at its edges so sections flow into each other): About `about-ivory-mountains.webp` (ivory marble, fine gold veins, a small peak lower right), Selected Work `projects-ivory-mountain-frame.webp` (navy rock framing, mirrored to upper-left and lower-right, the layer wider than the section so the text stays on clean paper), Tech expertise `tech-stack-ivory-mountains.webp` (ivory marble, navy mountains with gold veins, mirrored into the bottom-right corner so the text stays on clean marble; particle dots navy like the rest; `tint="brass"` on Showcase / `data-tint` stays available), What I offer `services-ivory-marble.webp` (a compact chapter, `compact` on Showcase; marble with navy rock in the top-left and bottom-right corners, covering the section), the closing statement `closing-ivory-mountain-left.webp` (`.bg-closing`: marble with gold veins, mountains on the left behind an AI chip shape ("ai" in shapes.ts) in navy dots; one short heading "Experience decides what to build." and two plain sentences on the right), Contact and footer one block (`Contact.astro`, `.bg-contact`, `contact-footer-mountain-left.webp`: my contact marble mirrored so the mountain range rises on the left; contact details and the footer line on the right, the particle envelope on the left; the home page passes `footer={false}` to Base so there is no gap; other pages keep `Footer.astro` with the same picture as `.footer-bg`). Pictures that sit at the bottom of a section use `inset: auto 0 0` with the image's aspect-ratio, so their fade always starts at the picture's own top edge. On phones the projects and services frames are left out. Layers behind the content: silk -3, section pictures -2, particles -1, so the particle shapes always form on top of the pictures; sections with a picture and their ancestors must not create a stacking context (no isolate, z-index, opacity or `transition:animate` on `<main>`). `html` has `overflow-x: clip`. Showcases take the class as `background`. A fixed full-page WebGL background (`src/scripts/silk.ts`) renders stone or velvet and eases between them; chapters carry `data-theme="ivory" | "midnight"`, `motion.ts` switches `<html data-theme>` at mid-screen and the text palette follows via CSS variables (`--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--panel`, `--contrast`, `--accent`). `Seam.astro` (brass star on a hairline) marks each change of material. Fonts self-hosted via @fontsource-variable: Bodoni Moda (display; the hero name at weight 600) + Geist (body). Sharp corners. The hero art `public/hero-art.webp` (my cut-out photo composited over the navy marble with gold veins and a thin gold circle from my mockup, the old figure inpainted away) was upscaled 4x with Real-ESRGAN and made transparent where the paper was, so they sit on the page's own paper. Motion: one layer in `src/scripts/motion.ts` (GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis), hooks `data-split`, `data-rule`, `.reveal` / `.reveal-item`, `data-enter`, `.shield`, `data-showcase` (chapters that cycle through their items on their own, `src/scripts/showcase.ts`). Header: KM monogram, the chapter links, "Let's talk" on the right; transparent at the top of the page, glass after, hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up, marks the chapter on screen; in-page links scroll smoothly (capture-phase click handler). The home page opens exactly like my mockup: `Hero.astro` (eyebrow, name with the surname in brass, line, "View my work" and "About me", the art on the right whose bottom edge is the diagonal cream cut, the stack listed on the marble, a scroll mark on the left; as tall as the art, so About already shows below it; no particles), then `About.astro` (01, "About" navy and "Me" brass italic, three short paragraphs incl. security, a hairline divider, four values with line icons in a 2x2 grid: clean code, security/roles/rights, Azure & DevOps, continuous improvement), then `Facts.astro` (brass counters counting with the scroll over a particle globe, `globe@0.12`, that fades in as the section arrives). A plain section that takes the cloud back (also the first one) freezes what is on screen and forms its scene from there. Particles (`src/scripts/particles.ts`): `[data-shapes]` sections become scroll keyframes, showcases flow the cloud into each item's scene; `data-faint` / `data-faint-until` keep a section's cloud hidden or faint up to a point; dots are midnight on stone and brass on velvet; a page without scenes shows none. Particle cloud: 9000 points on wide screens, 4500 on phones; showcase flows take 1.8 s and 2 s (items every 6 s, Work 7 s); plain sections form their scene over the first quarter of their scroll. Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content lives in `content/`; never hardcode case-study text in components.
- Copy rules: no em/en dashes in visible text (use commas, colons, periods, or a plain hyphen for ranges); the hero eyebrow, chapter numbers and the scroll mark from my mockup are intended.
- Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages (static). Keep the build host-agnostic.

## Code conventions
- Simple, readable code. No clever abstractions.
- No inline styles. Tailwind utility classes or scoped component CSS.
- Small components, one responsibility each.
- Type all frontmatter with Astro content collections (`src/content.config.ts`).
- English UI. Slovenian translation may come later; keep strings in content, not in markup.

## Content rules
- The transport / logistics field I work in now is never named (I cannot take projects in that field): the e-CMR work is shown in general terms as `document-platform.md` ("Digital document exchange platform"), the ReBAC work as "Security, roles and rights for a B2B platform"; the carrier import stays hidden (`_carrier-data-import.md`, skipped by the collections). The apartment site is not named ("Holiday apartment website").
- Professional work (Trinet) is described anonymously: no client names, no screenshots with real data, no internal system names beyond what is in `content/professional/`.
- Side projects may link to GitHub repos.
- AI-assisted scaffolding (Lovable) is disclosed where used.

## Pages
- `/` is the whole site, one scroll: `#home` hero -> `#about` 01 About me -> facts -> `#work` 02 Selected Work (ivory) -> seam -> `#skills` 03 Tech Expertise (ivory) -> seam -> `#offer` 04 What I Offer (ivory) -> `#closing` AI statement -> `#contact` contact and footer in one block. The three chapters are `Showcase.astro` (number, two-line title, lead, clickable index, one item on show with arrows, the cloud forming its scene on the right, advancing on its own).
- Scenes are named in `src/scripts/shapes.ts` (drawn primitives) and `src/scripts/particles.ts` (tech marks). Skills come from `content/skills.json`, services from `content/services.json` (each with a `shape`), work from the case-study collections (`label` is the short index name) with the scene per slug set in `index.astro`.
- `/work/[slug]` detail pages still exist for the professional case studies (linked as "Full case study" when a study has no live site or repo).
